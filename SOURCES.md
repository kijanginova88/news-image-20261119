## 3. World Court rules today on Rohingya genocide (World / Justice)
- The International Court of Justice delivers its judgment on
  Thursday 19 November at 10:00 in The Hague in The Gambia v.
  Myanmar, on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the
  Rohingya. The case was filed in November 2019; the 2017 military
  campaign drove more than 700,000 Rohingya into Bangladesh. The
  judgment is final, with no appeal. The court has never before found
  a state responsible for genocide.
